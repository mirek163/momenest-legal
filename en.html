Privacy policy

In effect from September 21, 2026

This describes what data about you Momenest processes, why, where it is kept, who can see it, how long we keep it and what your rights are. Momenest does not sell data, does not use it for advertising and does not track what you do outside the app.

Who is responsible for the data

The controller of personal data is the operator of the Momenest app: Miroslav Baša. You can write to them straight from the app (Settings → Help and feedback) or by e-mail at momenest.support@gmail.com.

Without an account, everything stays on the phone

Momenest can be used without an account. Chores, rewards, the garden, settings and everything else are then stored only on this phone and are not sent anywhere. Something leaves the phone only when you do it yourself: you send a message to support, turn on error reports, use the barcode scanner or add something to Google Calendar — each of these is described below. Uninstalling the app deletes the data on the phone.

What we store when you have an account

An account is for backup, groups and friends. With an account we store on the server:

  • account details: your e-mail address and account identifier; a password only in unreadable (hashed) form. When you sign in with Google, Google also passes on your name and a link to the profile photo of your Google account — the app does not use them, but the sign-in service stores them;
  • your profile: a name or nickname, an avatar and a profile photo if you upload one. The photo is made smaller on the phone first, and its metadata, location included, is removed;
  • what you create in the app: chores and their notes, completions and skips, rewards and wishes, routines, events, things at home (for example the purchase date and the end of the warranty), shopping lists with their items, prices and trips, days away (dates only, no reason) and the garden;
  • game progress: experience, coins, streaks, achievements and the group’s activity history;
  • the app’s settings;
  • friends, friend requests, blocked people and invitations to groups;
  • when you accepted the terms of use, and which version.

Drinking log — health data

When you turn on the drinking log, Momenest stores what and how much you drank, your daily goal and, if there is one, a drinking instruction from your doctor. This is health data, so we treat it with particular care: only you can see it — nobody in a group, not even its administrator. We process it only with your explicit consent, which you give by turning the feature on and entering the data yourself. You can withdraw that consent at any time: turn the drinking log off and delete the entries — one at a time in the drinks overview, all at once by deleting your account, or on request. The drinking log is meant for adults; a group member with the role Child does not see it.

Who can see your data

  • Members of a group see what belongs to the group: chores, who finished what, rewards, wishes, events, things at home, shopping lists, the activity history, and your name, avatar and profile photo. A private shopping list is seen only by you.
  • Nobody else sees your own space.
  • Friends, and anyone you send a friend request to, see your name and avatar, not your photo. Only somebody who knows your friend code can find you, and somebody you have blocked cannot find you at all.
  • Nobody but you sees the drinking log.
  • The operator accesses data on the server only when that is needed to run the service, fix an error, handle your request or a report, or when the law requires it.

We do not sell, rent or pass data to anyone else for advertising.

Support, feedback and reports

When you write to support or send feedback, we store the message, a reply e-mail if you fill one in, a screenshot if you attach one, and technical details: the app version, the system and its version, the phone model, the language, the appearance and the part of the app you are writing from. Diagnostics are attached only when you tick them yourself; they contain error codes and kinds, never chore content, names or e-mail addresses. Without an account, the message is linked only to a random installation code, in a form from which the code cannot be worked out — it is used only to protect against abuse.

When you report a person or content, we store the report and the account it concerns. Reports are read by a person.

Error reports

Error reports are off until you turn them on in Settings. When they are on, the app sends Sentry the kind of error, the place in the code, the screen (without what was on it), the app version and whether you were signed in. Never a chore title, a note, a name, an e-mail address, an account identifier, or what you did and when. They can be turned off at any time, with immediate effect.

Scanner, Google Calendar and notifications

The barcode scanner uses the camera only when you open it. The code is recognised by the Google ML Kit library on the phone itself; the camera image is neither sent nor stored, and the code read is saved with the product in the shopping list. The ML Kit library does, however, send Google technical data — about the device and the app, an installation identifier, response times and error codes — for diagnostics and usage statistics.

When you choose to add an event or a chore to Google Calendar, your calendar opens with the name, date and note filled in. Google receives these details when the calendar opens, and only you save them; from then on Google’s policies apply to them.

Reminders and notifications are created by the app on the phone itself. No server sends them to you.

Why we process data

  • The account, backup, groups, friends, support and everything the app does when you use it: performance of a contract, that is the terms of use (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • The drinking log: your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR).
  • Error reports: your consent, given by turning them on in Settings (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).
  • Security and protection against abuse — limits on the number of messages, blocking, reports, server logs — and the ML Kit library’s technical data: a legitimate interest in a secure, working service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Who processes the data, and where

  • Supabase (Supabase, Inc.) — the database, sign-in and file storage. The data is kept in a data centre in Ireland, in the EU. Supabase also sends the e-mails that confirm an account and change a password.
  • Google — sign-in with a Google account and the ML Kit library in the scanner.
  • Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) — error reports, and only when you turn them on.

Supabase, Google and Sentry are American companies or belong to one. Where data leaves the EU, this happens under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission.

How long we keep data

  • The account and everything that belongs to it, until you delete the account.
  • When you leave a group, what you did in it stays in its history, with your name — for as long as the group exists or until you delete your account.
  • Support requests and reports, for as long as we need them to handle them and to protect others from repeated breaches of the rules, but no longer than 2 years after they are resolved. Your own requests are deleted with your account.
  • The server’s connection logs (for example the IP address and time) are deleted automatically within a few days.
  • You delete the data on the phone by uninstalling the app.

Deleting your account

You delete your account in the app: Settings → Delete account. If you no longer have the app, write to momenest.support@gmail.com from the address the account is linked to, and we will delete the account within 30 days at the latest.

Deleting the account removes your sign-in details, your profile and profile photo, your memberships of groups and everything tied to you — completions, coins, experience, wishes, days away, private shopping lists, the activity history by you and about you, and the drinking log — as well as your friends and blocks, the record of accepting the terms and your support requests, screenshots included. Groups you founded disappear entirely, for the other members too.

Only what belongs to somebody else’s group remains: chores, routines, events, things at home and shopping items you added to it — without your name. And reports that somebody else made about you, without a link to your account.

Your rights

You have the right of access to your data and to a copy of it, to rectification, to erasure, to restriction of processing, to data portability, and the right to object to processing based on legitimate interest. You can withdraw consent at any time; what was processed before remains lawful. Just write straight from the app (Settings → Help and feedback) or by e-mail at momenest.support@gmail.com — we will answer within one month at the latest. If you believe we handle your data unlawfully, you can complain to the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (www.uoou.gov.cz).

Children

Children can use Momenest too. A child aged 15 or over may create an account of their own; a younger child only with the consent of a parent or other legal guardian. A parent can ask at any time for access to their child’s data or for the child’s account to be deleted. If we learn that the account of a child under 15 was created without a parent’s consent, we delete it. In a group, the administrator can give a member the role Child; that member does not see the features meant only for adults, such as the drinking log.

Security

The connection to the server is encrypted. Your sign-in is kept on the phone in the system’s secure storage. On the server everybody reaches only their own data and that of their groups — rules in the database itself enforce this, not just the app. Profile photos and screenshots are kept in non-public storage.

Changes to this policy

When the policy changes, you will find the new version in the app with a new date, and you will learn of a significant change in What’s new.
